# Privacy Policy — rabbita-agent

This is a personal tool used only by its developer to let a self-hosted
assistant read and manage the developer's own Google Calendar.

- **Data accessed:** events in the developer's own Google Calendar.
- **Use:** only to fulfil the developer's own requests (list, create, update events).
- **Processing:** event content may be sent to the developer's chosen LLM
  provider to process a request.
- **Storage:** OAuth tokens are kept on the developer's private server.
  Calendar data is not retained beyond what a request needs.
- **Sharing:** no data is sold or shared with anyone else.
- **Contact:** malarabbitta@gmail.com
